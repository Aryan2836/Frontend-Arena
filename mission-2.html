    Mission 02 / HTML foundations

    Meaningful HTML

    Choose elements by meaning so browsers, assistive technology and developers understand the content.

    Easy50 XPText semanticsAccessibility

    Reference

    Appearance and meaning are different

    <strong> and <em>

    Strong importance and spoken emphasis carry meaning beyond visual style.

    <b> and <i>

    Draw attention or use an alternate voice without adding strong importance.

    <mark>

    Marks content that is relevant in the current context.

    <del> and <ins>

    Represent removed and added content.

    <p><strong>Important:</strong> Save your work.</p> <p>Search matched <mark>JavaScript</mark>.</p>
